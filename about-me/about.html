ABOUT ME

==============================

Hey there! My name is Ezra, and I am a Digital Library Analyst for the Lowcountry Digital Library (LCDL) at the College of Charleston.

I previously worked as a Research Assistant with Caitlin Hudac on the Social Connection and Well-Being (SCWB) Study, and I am a 2023 alumnus from SUNY Oswego. See more of my previous experience here

I'm in a constant pursuit of knowledge, chasing after the answer to the question: "What is it like?" I hope to use my knowledge, education, and professional skills to promote open access of knowledge to all!

My professional interests include transcriptions, coding (typically updating this website), and Excel (hence the skills page theming). Feel free to reach out about my passion for Excel– I'd be glad to talk about my collection of spreadsheets!

My non-professional interests and hobbies include (but are not limited to): Medical History and now-defunct medical procedures, listening to music, dinosaurs, cooking/making new recipes, drawing, hiking, and engaging with horror movies/media.

Thanks for stopping by!
