New updates for new and ongoing projects. Pertinent life updates, such as graduate education, awards, honors, and career updates.

PROJECTS

During undergraduate studies, an honors thesis was completed on artificial intelligence ethics, specifically the usage of AI and computer vision for facial recognition. The study was completed in the context of gender identity and gender modality. Other projects completed while pursuing undergraduate education include: a paper on embodied cognition and aphantasia, a poster on narcissistic traits, and presentations in French on flora and fauna, AIDS, and notable people in medieval France. Post-graduation, projects were completed at the University of South Carolina during employment as a neuroscience research assistant. One such project is the Social Connection and Well-Being (SCWB) study, an NIH R01-funded study using EEG (electroencephalography) to explore how social connection and well-being changes across time in adolescents. Related to a task in the SCWB Study, a JavaScript-based quiz was created for a poster presentation at an international conference for conference-goers to interact with a mock version of the task. Outside of EEG and neuroscience, a project was completed with Michelle Brown via manuscript preparation for a study on maltreatment and abuse in adolescents.
